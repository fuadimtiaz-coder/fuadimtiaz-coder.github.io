Our History

Government Laboratory High School has a long and important history in the educational landscape of Dhaka. The institution was established in 1961. Its development was closely connected with teacher education and practical teaching. The school became a place where students could receive education while also contributing to the practical training environment of teachers.

Since its establishment, the school has passed through many stages of development. Generations of students have entered its classrooms, learned from their teachers and eventually moved forward into different professions and areas of society. Each generation has added another chapter to the story of the institution.

The history of a school cannot be understood only by looking at dates. It also includes the people who taught there, the students who studied there, the activities that took place on the campus, the traditions that developed and the memories carried by former students. For this reason, this website aims to preserve both the documented history and the wider heritage of the school.

Over the decades, Bangladesh has experienced enormous social, technological and educational changes. Schools have also changed with the country. Classrooms, teaching methods, learning resources, examinations and student activities have developed over time. Through these changes, educational institutions have continued their central mission of preparing young people for the future.

Government Laboratory High School remains associated with the educational history of Dhaka. Its long existence means that many generations have a personal connection with the institution. Former students often remember their teachers, classmates, classrooms, playgrounds, examinations, cultural programs and school events long after leaving the campus.

Education

Education is at the heart of every school. At Government Laboratory High School, academic learning forms an important part of student life. Students attend classes, study different subjects, complete assignments and prepare for examinations while developing the knowledge and skills required for their future.

A successful educational environment depends on many people. Teachers guide students through lessons and help them understand difficult subjects. Students contribute through their curiosity, questions, effort and participation. Parents and guardians also play an important role by supporting students outside the classroom.

Modern education is not limited to memorizing information. Students also need communication skills, creativity, critical thinking, discipline, teamwork and the ability to solve problems. School life provides opportunities for students to develop these qualities through both academic and extracurricular activities.

Campus and Facilities

The school campus is an important part of student life. A school environment includes classrooms, learning spaces, laboratories, libraries, playgrounds and areas where students can participate in different activities.

Laboratories are particularly important in science education because they allow students to connect theoretical knowledge with practical experience. A library is equally valuable because it provides access to books and encourages students to develop independent reading and research habits.

The physical environment of a school also creates memories. Students remember the classrooms where they studied, the corridors where they met friends, the playground where they participated in sports and the locations where important school events took place.

Student Life and Activities

School life is not only about textbooks and examinations. Students also need opportunities to express themselves, discover interests and work together. Cultural programs, sports, clubs, competitions, celebrations and other activities can help students develop confidence and teamwork.

Sports can teach discipline, cooperation and perseverance. Cultural activities can encourage creativity and appreciation of music, literature, art and performance. Clubs and student organizations can provide opportunities for students to develop leadership and communication skills.

National occasions and school events can also create a strong sense of community. Students, teachers and former students can come together to celebrate important moments and preserve traditions.

Achievements

The achievements of a school are created by its students, teachers, staff and wider community. Academic results are an important part of a school's record, but achievement can take many forms.

Students may represent their school in sports competitions, cultural programs, debates, science activities, creative contests and other events. Every successful student contributes to the reputation and heritage of the institution.

Former students also contribute to the legacy of a school when they succeed in professional, academic, social and creative fields. Their stories can inspire younger students and demonstrate how education can influence a person's future.

Heritage and Legacy

The greatest legacy of a school is its people. Thousands of students can pass through an institution over many decades, and each group leaves behind memories and experiences that become part of the school's identity.

Teachers are an especially important part of that legacy. A teacher's influence can continue long after a student has left school. Lessons, advice, discipline and encouragement can shape students' decisions and careers for many years.

Alumni also form an important connection between the past and the present. Former students can preserve old photographs, documents, stories and memories that might otherwise disappear. Collecting these materials can help future generations understand what school life was like in earlier periods.

This website is intended to become a growing digital archive of the school's history. As reliable historical information, photographs, documents and memories are collected, additional sections can be added to make the archive larger and more complete.

Future of This Website

This is only the beginning. The website can eventually contain separate pages for the complete history, former headteachers, teachers and staff, academic records, student activities, sports, clubs, publications, alumni, historical photographs, important events and many other subjects.

A detailed historical timeline can also be developed so that visitors can explore the school's journey year by year. Old photographs can be placed beside modern photographs to show how the campus and school life have changed over time.

The ultimate goal is to create a large digital heritage project that students, teachers, alumni, parents and visitors can use to learn about Government Laboratory High School and its contribution to education in Dhaka.
